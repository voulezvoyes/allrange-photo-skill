# AllrangeKit photo examples

The fictional-person commercial images below are style examples, **not** claims about real customers or actual AllrangeKit product usage.

## 1. Website hero / banner
**Input:** [사진 용도] 홈페이지 배너 / [인물] 30대 흑인 남성 1명 / [행동] 아침에 거실 소파에서 휴대폰을 보며 잠깐 생각함 / [장소] 집 / [글씨 넣을 공간] 오른쪽 / [카메라 구도] 자동

**Selected composition:** environmental medium-wide scene, eye-level three-quarter angle, subject on left third, right half clean enough for typography.

**Prompt:**
```text
Create a photorealistic, candid commercial editorial photograph for a contemporary health and wellbeing brand website. One fictional adult Black man in his early 30s sits comfortably on a lived-in living-room sofa in the morning, quietly checking his phone while thinking, not posing for the camera. Observational three-quarter eye-level view, medium-wide framing that leaves clean, calm negative space in the right half for a website headline. Soft window daylight with believable domestic shadows, natural skin texture, realistic fingers and clothing folds, restrained color and modest depth of field. The home should feel real, comfortable, and private rather than like a luxury showroom. Do not depict an STI diagnosis or suggest the person is a real customer. No visible branding, legible writing, UI screens, packaging or test results. Avoid glossy skin, distorted hands, exaggerated smiles, fake lens flare, and sterile corporate-stock aesthetics.
```
